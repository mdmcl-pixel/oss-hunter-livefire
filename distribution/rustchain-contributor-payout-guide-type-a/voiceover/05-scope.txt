The public review guidelines are straightforward: keep the pull request focused, test locally, update documentation when behavior changes, and avoid unrelated changes. For miner files, there is an extra reproducibility rule: four miner artifacts are pinned by SHA 256 in miners slash checksums dot sha256. If one changes, the manifest must be regenerated in the same commit or CI will fail even if thousands of other tests pass. This is a good example of why evidence matters more than a large diff. A small change with the exact focused test is easier to review and safer to merge.
